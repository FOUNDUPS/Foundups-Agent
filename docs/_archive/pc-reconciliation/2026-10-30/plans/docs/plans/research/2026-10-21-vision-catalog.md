## Vision synthesis and conflicts that affect contracts

### Durable intended behavior

The five primary vision documents agree on composable autonomous venture operations, evidence of beneficial contribution, participant rewards, shared infrastructure, anti-hoarding circulation, Bitcoin reserve anchoring, and cross-FoundUp cooperation. The 2026 framing adds a clearer product path: RedDog conversation and scoped Memex, OpenClaw supervision, WRE work authority/verification, bounded Hermes or DAE execution, and p.fMALL discovery (`docs/foundups_vision.md:56-77`).

The blueprint's newer UCA section separates:

- **Compute Award (`ca`):** productive agent output, associated with the FoundUp-specific award/stake unit.
- **Compute Reward (`cr`):** human verification, validation, governance, and beneficial participation.
- **Compute Dividend (`cd`):** ongoing flow from staked participation through Return on Compute.

Evidence: `WSP_knowledge/docs/FoundUps_0102_Vision_Blueprint.md:105-130`, `UCA = ca + cr + cd`. A contribution record should identify which flow it proves; one undifferentiated “earned token” counter would obscure economic policy.

## Source authority and economic readiness
