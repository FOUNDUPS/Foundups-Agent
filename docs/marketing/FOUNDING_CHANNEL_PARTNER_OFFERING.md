# Founding Channel Partner Offering

**Status**: Pre-Launch Registration Open
**Registry**: Closes at Mainnet Genesis
**Hard Cap**: 21 Founding Channel Partners

---

## What This Is

The Founding Channel Partner program is a Genesis-level protocol participation surface for distribution partners who contribute channel access, visibility, and market reach to the pAVS ecosystem.

Founding Channel Partners are registered before mainnet launch and receive protocol allocations from a bounded, deterministic pool governed by transparent on-chain rules.

**This is protocol participation, not investment.**

---

## Key Mechanics

### Hard Cap

The Founding Channel Partner registry is limited to **21 partners maximum**. This cap is encoded in the protocol and cannot be changed after deployment.

### Pre-Launch Only

Registration is available **only before mainnet launch**. The registry closes permanently at the first mainnet FoundUp genesis event (token issuance). No additional partners can be added after closure.

### Equal Split Allocation

In the Genesis tranche, all registered Founding Channel Partners receive **equal allocations** from the Channel Partner Pool. There is no tiered weighting or preferential treatment based on registration order, contribution size, or other factors.

### Protocol-Governed Distribution

Allocations are determined by deterministic protocol rules:

- Channel Partner Pool is a bounded partition of the Du pool
- Du pool represents 4% of total epoch distributions
- Channel Partner Pool splits the PassiveParticipationPool with BTC Stakers
- Distribution occurs per epoch according to protocol mechanics
- All rules are transparent and verifiable on-chain

### Permanent Closure

Once the registry closes at mainnet genesis:

- No new Founding Channel Partners can be added
- The closure event is recorded immutably
- Registered partners continue to receive allocations per protocol rules
- The founding cohort becomes a closed, finite set

## Protocol Allocation Structure

The Du pool allocation structure:

```
Total Epoch Distribution (100%)
├── Stakeholder Pools (80%)
│   ├── Un Pool (60%) - Active engagement
│   ├── Dao Pool (16%) - Active work
│   └── Du Pool (4%) - Protocol participants
│       ├── ActiveFounderPool (80% of Du)
│       └── PassiveParticipationPool (20% of Du)
│           ├── BTCStakerPool (weighted allocation)
│           └── ChannelPartnerPool (equal split) ← YOU ARE HERE
└── Network Pools (20%)
    ├── Network Pool (16%) - Drip rewards
    └── Fund Pool (4%) - Protocol operations
```

Founding Channel Partners participate in the ChannelPartnerPool, which is a bounded partition of the PassiveParticipationPool within the Du pool.
