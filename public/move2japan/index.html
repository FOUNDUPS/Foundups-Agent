What is included in this collection?

Five manually reviewed homes from Fukui Prefecture’s akiya bank, with source pages checked on 17 October 2026. This is an initial collection, not a live feed or a complete inventory. Photographs and tours are available on the original pages. The source’s building-area label is retained in the details; it should not be assumed to mean total usable floor area.

For property owners and real estate agencies

Owner and agency registration is the next planned step. Public submissions are not open here yet. The publishing service will need account verification, authority to advertise the property, permission to use photographs, and a review before a listing appears.
