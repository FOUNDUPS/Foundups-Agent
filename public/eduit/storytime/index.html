Little Shrew

Tap a shrew to wake it; sleepy parents need more taps.
Tap the green vine to guide it to shelter.

THE STORY

Watch the story

A faint tremor wakes a little shrew. It follows its nose to a root pocket—just before a giant foot presses into the earth.

For grown-ups: play together. This fictional scene includes a stomp and a collapsed burrow; no injury is shown. A missed tap simply gives another try. Practice mode removes the time limit.
