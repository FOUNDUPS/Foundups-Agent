## Core proposal to test

Ancestral hominoids may have been isolated in coastal/island environments in the Socotra–Gulf of Aden region; changing shorelines, foraging ecology, competition and eventual dispersal may have contributed to unusual evolutionary pressures. The original account proposes two major marine/geological transitions. Preserve the proposal as a **hypothesis**, not a conclusion.

## Critical tests and known issues

1. **Chronology:** Messinian Salinity Crisis (~5.97–5.33 Ma) and Zanclean reflooding (~5.33 Ma) occurred in the Mediterranean basin. A hydrological impact on the Gulf of Aden must be established independently; it is not a documented consequence of the Zanclean flood.
2. **Paleogeography:** Verify Socotra's tectonic evolution, regional bathymetry, uplift/subsidence, and proposed islands southeast of Socotra. "Ourobora Isles" is the author's hypothetical name, not an established geological designation.
3. **Sea level:** Distinguish basin-specific Mediterranean drawdown from global sea level and local tectonic changes. Do not infer a kilometer of global sea-level rise.
4. **Biology:** Evaluate Proconsul-era hominoid dispersal, comparative anatomy, and aquatic-foraging explanations against fossil, genetic, and paleoenvironmental evidence. Aquatic Ape explanations are not the mainstream consensus.
5. **Archaeology:** Verify alleged Homo erectus/Homo habilis remains on Socotra; do not treat stone tools as identified hominin fossils.
6. **Etymology:** Do not assume Aden derives from Eden or means birthplace of humanity.
7. **Falsifiability:** Specify dated, geographically explicit predictions that would distinguish this scenario from competing hypotheses.
