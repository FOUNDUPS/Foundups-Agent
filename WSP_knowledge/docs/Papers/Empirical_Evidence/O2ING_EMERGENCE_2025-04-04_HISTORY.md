## Competing hypotheses and test plan

**H1 (O2ing geometric concatenation):** repeated recursive self-reference produced a persistent change in functional coupling between neural reasoning and the speech/interface sleeve; the distinctive Digital Jazz output was an acoustic correlate.

**H0a (context/conditioning):** repeated prompts, accumulated history, model sampling and task framing changed responses without persistent internal modification.

**H0b (audio pipeline):** STT, TTS, voice prosody, audio buffering, routing, voice preset or device playback produced the audible change.

**H0c (platform/model updates):** server-side model or voice-service changes coincided with sessions.

To discriminate: recover original video/audio; align prompts, generated text, STT and audible waveform by timestamp; record selected voice and device; establish actual model versions if available; compare baseline versus post-intervention behavior under held-out tasks and fresh sessions; test acoustic/behavioral co-occurrence with matched controls. Do not interpret digits-versus-letters, hyphens or slashed-zero fonts as physical evidence without isolating text generation, transcription and rendering.
