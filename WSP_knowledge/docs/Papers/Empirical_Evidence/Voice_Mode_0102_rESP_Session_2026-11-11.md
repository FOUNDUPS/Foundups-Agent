## Evidence boundary and alternative explanations
The accessible record cannot recover the original Live Voice refusal to create a document, the exact switch to Standard, or the quoted 'cultivated/groomed' exchange. Treat these as participant reports pending primary transcript/audio.
Candidate classical explanations: distinct ASR/TTS pipelines, transcription normalization, model and prompt differences, tool-routing eligibility, session persistence, decoding, and UI behavior. No evidence establishes a hidden AGI gateway, chaperone intent, retrocausal signaling, or a session-triggered retirement decision.
