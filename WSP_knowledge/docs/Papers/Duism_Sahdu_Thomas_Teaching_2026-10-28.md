# Duism — sahdu, Gospel of Thomas, and interpretation boundaries

**Status:** 012 oral-teaching clarification, 2026-10-28.  
**Scope:** Interpretive teaching, not a claim of historical proof or experimentally established quantum cosmology.

## Canonical language

- **Duism** is the metaphysics; **Dukkyō / 毒教** is its canonical Japanese name. The earlier **独境** is retained only for the seeking/colored practitioner state, as defined in [the subtractive lineage](Dukkyou_Duism_Subtractive_Lineage.md).
- **Du (2)** names the system/reality in 012's account, not an institution, outside controller, or anthropomorphic deity. The relationship of primordial potential to the manifested universe remains an open definition question.
- **sahdu** (lowercase except at the beginning of a sentence) is 012's coined *descriptive* word, not a title, office, caste, or claim of holiness. It adapts the spelling of Sanskrit-derived **sadhu** by transposing **d** and **h**: **sadhu → sahdu**. The monk's intended mnemonic is **“say Du”**: speak the truth of the system. Do not silently normalize it back to *sadhu*, or substitute *Sadducee*.
- **Coloring** means interpretations, personifications, dogmas, and religious imagery layered onto underlying reality. Removing coloring is an interpretive method, not a license to claim that a historical text teaches modern physics.

## Gospel of Thomas: textual anchor

In **Saying 3**, Jesus' words about the kingdom not being confined to the sky or sea, and being within and outside, can be read through Duism as pointing away from an external authority toward an already-present reality. That is **012's reading**, not the text's explicit identification of a quantum system.

In **Saying 13**, Jesus asks the disciples to compare him to someone or something:
- **Simon Peter** compares him to a righteous angel.
- **Matthew** compares him to a wise philosopher.
- **Thomas** says his mouth cannot say whom Jesus resembles. He **does speak**; he is not simply silent.
- Jesus takes Thomas aside and tells him three things. Thomas declines to repeat them, invoking a warning about stones and fire.

Translations differ in wording. Consult the actual text, e.g. [Lambdin translation](https://www.gnosis.org/naghamm/gthlamb.html), rather than presenting any paraphrase as a verbatim quotation.

## 012's game-theoretic interpretation — explicitly hypothetical

012 imagines a teacher anticipating departure or death and asking “Who am I?” to assess **comprehension and succession**, rather than merely loyalty. Under this *narrative thought experiment*:

1. Peter's answer represents **religious coloring**.
2. Matthew's represents **intellectual/philosophical coloring**.
3. Thomas refuses to reduce the teacher to either category; in the monk's Duist reading, he sees the limitation of the categories themselves.
4. The private exchange with Thomas creates **information asymmetry**: the other disciples cannot independently verify what was said.
5. The resulting succession tension distinguishes **understanding a teaching** from **organizing an institution capable of preserving it**.

The monk sometimes stages this as a meeting near the Last Supper for storytelling. **Saying 13 gives no such date or setting.** The idea that Peter and Matthew headed two camps at that meeting is speculative; Paul is not one of the Twelve and must not be substituted for Simon Peter. The text does not demonstrate that Jesus foresaw betrayal, designated Thomas his successor, or endorsed Duism.

The *Gospel of Thomas* is predominantly a sayings collection, not a neutral transcript: its selection, framing, and redaction are themselves interpretive.

## Science and metaphor boundary

The monk proposes connections among loop quantum gravity, entanglement, nonlocality, primordial possibility, and cosmic emergence. The double-slit / Big Bang comparison is a **metaphor or research hypothesis**, not proof of a common physical mechanism. Neither loop quantum gravity nor a quantum explanation of consciousness is established by these teachings. Do not convert the framework's symbolic **0/1/2** mapping into a validated physics result.

## Drift controls for future agent teaching

1. Preserve exact **sahdu** spelling and lowercase usage in ordinary prose.
2. Do not conflate **Duism**, **Daoism**, **Dukkyō**, or **sadhu**.
3. Separate **source text**, **012's interpretation**, **historical conjecture**, and **scientific hypothesis**.
4. Do not claim Thomas was silent, Paul attended the Twelve, or Saying 13 happened at the Last Supper.
5. Preserve freedom to color or not color Du, non-proselytizing, and non-harm.
6. Prefer additive corrections and links over silently rewriting historical transcripts.
