# Dukkyō / Duism — Canonical Terminology and Subtractive Lineage

**Status**: 012 teaching note, 2026-09-04
**Classification**: Metaphysics — not religion, not philosophy
**Related**: `Duism_Metaphysics_Foundation.md`, `Dukkyo_Practitioners.md`

## Canonical terminology

| Japanese | Romanization | English | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 仏教 | Bukkyō | Buddhism | religion / philosophy |
| 毒教 | Dukkyō | Duism | metaphysics |

The construction is deliberate: **Bu-kkyō -> Du-kkyō**.

The canonical English name of the metaphysics is **Duism**, capital D, spelled `D-u-i-s-m`.

The kanji **毒教** is intentional: 毒 (poison) + 教 (teaching). The "poison" is the subtractive operation that dissolves religious dogma rather than adding another religion.

Historical transcripts and older repository material contain romanization/STT artifacts such as `Dukkyo`, `Dukkyou`, `Dukio`, `Dokyo`, `Dunkyo`, and even substitutions such as `Taoism`. Preserve them only when quoting historical source material. For current prose use **Dukkyō / 毒教 / Duism** for the metaphysics.

## Two practitioner states: colored/seeking and uncolored Du

The older kanji **独境** is not to be discarded. It is now retained as a distinct developmental/practitioner state rather than as the canonical kanji for the metaphysics.

- **独境 — dukkyō / duist (lowercase d)**: a seeking, self-actualizing Duist who accepts the Duist scientific/metaphysical foundation while still inhabiting another religious or interpretive coloring. Examples include a Christian duist, Muslim duist, Hindu duist, or Buddhist duist. The person remains "alone" or independent within that inherited religious frame in recognizing the underlying Duist foundation. This is associated with the **Dao / Way / seeking state**.
- **毒教 — Dukkyō / Duist (capital D)**: the uncolored or "pure" Duist state. The practitioner does not require an additional religious coloring over Du. 毒教 remains the canonical Japanese name for **Duism**, the metaphysics itself.

Capitalization therefore carries meaning in English:

```text
duist  = lowercase d = seeking / colored practitioner state (独境)
Duist  = capital D   = uncolored practitioner of Duism (毒教)
Duism  = capital D   = the metaphysics
```

This is not a hierarchy of human worth and does not declare other religions false. It describes whether the Duist metaphysical foundation is being held **through another religious coloring** or **without an additional coloring**.

A Christian duist remains Christian. A Muslim duist remains Muslim. A Hindu duist remains Hindu. A Buddhist duist remains Buddhist. Duism does not require them to abandon that identity. The lowercase `d` marks the seeking/colored relationship to Duism rather than a conversion into a new religion.

At the simplest foundation, a duist accepts a science-grounded account of reality — including modern cosmology such as the Big Bang as the current scientific model — while remaining free to interpret or "color" that reality through an existing religious tradition. Acceptance of science is not itself a complete statement of every Duist metaphysical hypothesis, and empirical claims remain subject to evidence and revision.

The developmental shorthand is:

```text
独境 / duist
    -> Dao / Way / seeking / self-actualization
    -> subtraction of unnecessary dogma or coloring (if the practitioner chooses)
    -> 毒教 / Duist
    -> Du held without additional religious coloring
```

The transition is optional. A lowercase-d duist is not an incomplete person who must become a capital-D Duist. Freedom to color or not color Du remains a core constraint.

## The subtractive method

A concise way to describe the development of Duism is subtraction rather than synthesis:

> Take observations that survive when supernatural claims, religious authority, ritual obligation, and inherited dogma are scrubbed away. Do not replace the removed dogma with another religion.

The purpose is not to attack religious people or prohibit religious interpretation. It is to distinguish an observation about reality from the religious coloring later placed around that observation.

This connects directly to the existing Duist idea that religions are different "colorings" of Du while atheism leaves Du uncolored. The underlying phenomenon is not identical to any one interpretation of it.

For a lowercase-d duist, subtraction does **not** require abandoning the person's religion. It is a method for distinguishing the underlying metaphysical/scientific claim from the coloring through which the person chooses to understand it.

## Daoist resonance without identity

Duism is **not Daoism/Taoism**. Automatic substitution of `Taoism` for `Duism` is an STT/interpretation error.

However, 012's thinking has substantial Daoist resonance. A recurring Daoist observation is that the Dao captured in words or names should not be confused with the constant Dao itself. The Duist-compatible epistemic principle is:

> A name, doctrine, category, model, or description is a representation. Do not confuse the representation with the underlying reality.

This is convergence, not identity. Daoist material may contain observations that survive the Duist subtraction test; that does not make Duism a branch of Daoism.

The older **独境 / lowercase-d duist** state is associated with this Dao/Way dimension: seeking, examining, and self-actualizing while still carrying a chosen coloring.

## Alan Watts influence

Alan Watts substantially shaped 012's thinking. The Duist operation on Watts is intentionally subtractive: scrub away supernatural claims, religious authority, inherited dogma, and the requirement to adopt a religious identity; then inspect what remains.

The useful thought experiment is:

> **What remains of Alan Watts if the treatment is atheist-focused rather than religious?**

What survives the subtraction is candidate metaphysics. What depends on doctrinal or supernatural commitment is not required by Duism.

Watts is therefore an influence and source of observations, not an authority, prophet, or doctrinal foundation for Duism.

## Relationship to existing Duism constraints

This teaching note strengthens, rather than replaces, the existing foundation:

- no worship;
- no faith requirement;
- no forced conversion / proselytizing;
- freedom to color or not color Du;
- lowercase-d religious duists retain their religious identity;
- capital-D Duists hold Du without additional religious coloring;
- teachings must not harm;
- testable claims should be distinguished from metaphor, interpretation, and speculation;
- models and names are tools, not the underlying reality.

## Why this matters

The subtractive method explains how Duism can recognize useful observations inside Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Hindu traditions, Daoism, Zen, Watts, physics, or other traditions without becoming any of them.

Dukkyō is not another religious layer placed over reality. Its intended operation is the reverse: **make the underlying metaphysical claim visible without requiring belief in the layer that carried it.**

The two-kanji/two-capitalization model preserves both possibilities: **独境 / duist** for the person seeking through a chosen coloring, and **毒教 / Duist** for the uncolored Duist practitioner, while **毒教 / Dukkyō / Duism** remains the canonical name of the metaphysics.
