# Launch a petition

Follow WSP 00/97. Read the repository-owned campaign skill at
`modules/platform_integration/social_media_orchestrator/skillz/foundup_campaign_operator/SKILLz.md`
and its petition procedure at
`modules/platform_integration/social_media_orchestrator/skillz/foundup_campaign_operator/references/petition-workflow.md`.

Retrieve the existing campaign before drafting. Reuse its stable identity and
platform draft; prepare finished copy and source evidence. Use human takeover
for account creation and the host's secure sign-in capability for credentials.
Verify actual signed-in access after handback; an ordinary external browser
link does not establish shared authentication. Stop repeating failed handoffs.

Apply current publication policy to the exact final copy and intended account.
Keep supporter records and credentials private. Verify saved drafts, public
petition URLs and any social posts independently. Report each stage truthfully.

Run `social campaign petition action=preview` or
`social campaign petition action=plan petition_url=<public_link>` through the
existing SOCIAL owner. A direct "RedDog, let's launch a petition ..." request
also returns this plan. Missing links remain pending; supplied links remain
unverified until actual readback. All platform entries default disabled.
No native account-creation/publication executor, production cadence or grant
is added. Do not repurpose the AntifaFM-only route or its station-specific grants.
