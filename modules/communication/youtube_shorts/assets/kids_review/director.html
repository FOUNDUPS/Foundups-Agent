Learning through play

EDUIT means Education Using Information Technology. Storytime teaches through a story, a game, and a short matching check: watch, play, verify.

EDUIT is the educational FoundUp within Play FoundUps. This first playable version uses code-driven animation. Optional AI characters are the next prototype; memberships come later.

Where it began

The monk recalls promoting the idea in 2004. The recovered project history records EDUIT in 2001 and its educational vision in 2007. The 2004 milestone remains a founder recollection.

Our evolutionary journey

The MAMMA characters are fictional. The mammal family tree has three living branches: MAMMA 🥚 (monotremes), MAMMA 🦘 (marsupials), and MAMMA 🤰 (placental mammals). They are branches, not stages where one turns into another. Our story follows the placental branch toward primates and humans.

A journey from mammaliaforms toward humans

Storytime follows a fictional family of little MAMMAs through a simplified learning journey inspired by mammalian evolution. Early mammaliaforms lived long before humans; they were not modern shrews. Our human story follows one later branch of the mammal family tree, not a direct chain through the pictured species.

The game grows with the learner

Imagine learning language, numbers, mathematics and science by playing a game. A child begins with sounds, words and counting; as the learner grows, the games and lessons become more advanced. Play earns points, and future EDUIT experiences may let learners use points within a safe educational game world.

EDUIT is the educational FoundUp. Storytime is its first learning experience; Evolution is one story path. This first tile is a prototype for reusable learning objects and the self-improving production system, not a complete autonomous learning platform.

Learning during a lifetime is different from biological evolution. Game points do not change real genes.

Watch. Notice. Help.

  1. Watch the story above.
  2. Tap a little shrew to wake it. Sleepy ones need more taps.
  3. Tap the green vine to guide it to safety.
  4. When the learning check is available, match the pictures to the sounds and words you heard.

Each arrival earns points. For a gentler pace, open ⋯ and turn on Untimed practice.

Spread two fingers or tap ⛶ to expand. Pinch together to return. The pencil freezes the game so a grown-up can draw a note.

For grown-ups

The story includes a rumble and a collapsing burrow. Practice mode keeps the shelter open.

Notice → wake → move is the first learning goal. A score is a game result, not a measure of a child's age or ability.
