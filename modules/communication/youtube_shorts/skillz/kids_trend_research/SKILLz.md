# Kids trend research

Read WSP 97 and the module README/INTERFACE. Use `src/kids_research.py` with an already-authorized injected YouTube service for bounded metadata snapshots. Use web research if that runtime is unavailable and label the method.

Collect dated public URLs, region/language, source channel, topic, publication date and actual observed counts. Read/watch/listen to accessible primary sources when relevant; label metadata-only evidence. Do not claim a media review from search snippets.

Compare time-separated matching observations with `rank_observed_growth`; retain baseline-only, missing-statistics, decreasing-counter and invalid-time labels. A ranking is neither a global trend nor evidence of educational quality. Never infer young viewers' individual profiles.

Propose at most three original 15-second learning/activity briefs; use one learning goal each, allow response time, and separate measured evidence from creative recommendations. Reuse neither recording nor characters without rights. Obtain independent educational/music review before production.

Record sources and retained outcomes through existing admitted WRE memory owners. Admit any recurring worker through the existing WRE scheduling/budget model, never a second daemon. ChatGPT scheduled research is a separate service and must be labeled as such. No paid generation or publishing is authorized by this research skill.
