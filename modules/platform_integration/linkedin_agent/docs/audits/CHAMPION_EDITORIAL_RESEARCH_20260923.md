## Editorial decision

Primary lane: **ROC**, with working question **Who owns the return on compute?**
Compare hardware location, equity/control, data and memory, customer relationships,
supplier portability and distribution of value. The conceptual overlap with the
proposed eSingularity.ai COGDC is local participation and benefit. Champion's
institutional/service model does not by itself specify a community-owned physical
data center, waste-heat reuse or the Fukui facility design.

No evidence retrieved establishes that II copied, adopted, endorsed or partnered
with FoundUps or eSingularity.ai. Do not write “models our COGDC” as fact. Existing
WSP 107 is FoundUps' own optional-II integration vision, not external validation.
Do not import II's nominal valuation/equity design into a Fukui financial proposal.

Candidate research priority: WSP 15 C3/I4/D4/Impact4 = 15/P1 for the ROC ownership
section. Evidence qualification precedes selection; the score is a research-work
priority, not a factual-confidence score. Define ROC and explain Champion before
comparison so readers can assess the distinction. Consider a short disclosed link
to the proposed eSingularity.ai regional-compute case after the comparison.

JHR may cite Champion only when a verified Japan-specific institution, deployment
or policy development adds material evidence. Good/Bad/Ugly needs actual operational
evidence; FoundUps needs a real implementation change. Avoid duplicating this story
across all four destinations.
