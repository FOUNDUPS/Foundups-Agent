## What the project is

YUMORI began with former Sukatto Land Kuzuryu in Fukui and describes a broader
regional asset-reuse and locally governed AI-compute initiative. The preparation
committee coordinates evidence, community discussion and project formation.
It is not established as the buyer, funded developer or operator of the entire
proposed facility. The formal implementation entity and administrative route
remain to be settled.

The proposal combines usable parts of the existing onsen/building with separate,
modular computing facilities on suitable parts of the site. Computing revenue,
after operating costs, debt service, tax and equipment replacement, could support
onsen/community functions if a viable surplus exists. Recoverable server heat
could supplement hot-water/heating demand if engineering supports it.

Education, research, local enterprise, agriculture/robotics, FoundUps teams,
culture and tourism are proposed uses. eSingularity supplies the education and
regional innovation context; FoundUps supplies the small-team, AI-assisted
venture-building context. A 24-hour onsen, startup floors, expanded outdoor baths,
cultural installations and national replication are visions subject to feasibility,
not completed facilities or confirmed partnerships.

"AI田んぼ" is an explanatory metaphor for compute capacity. "AI交番" describes a
nearby regional compute/service node; the project document explicitly says it
does not mean police authority or resident surveillance.
