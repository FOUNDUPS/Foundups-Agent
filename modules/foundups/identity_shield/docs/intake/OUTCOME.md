# Identity Shield - Outcome Definition

## User Outcome
A person can detect, verify, document, contain, and recover from identity-theft or impersonation events without repeatedly exposing more sensitive identity data to additional services. Identity Shield becomes a privacy-first defensive agent that helps the user establish what happened, what accounts or identities are at risk, and the next verified action to take.

## Desired End State
Identity Shield is a reusable consumer identity-defense FoundUp: a local-first PWA and agent workflow that can ingest suspicious calls/messages/notices, verify counterparties through trusted channels, build an evidence timeline, guide account containment and reporting, and coordinate approved actions across relevant institutions while preserving user control of raw identity data.

## Success Metrics
- Raw high-risk identity data remains local by default and remote transmission is explicit, minimal, and consented.
- The PoC can classify a suspected identity event and produce a safe, auditable response plan without exposing secrets.
- Counterparty verification distinguishes a claimed organization/contact from an independently verified channel.
- Evidence events are timestamped and exportable for banks, credit bureaus, telecoms, platforms, insurers, law enforcement, or cybersecurity responders as applicable.
- High-risk actions require explicit user authorization and cannot be self-authorized by the agent.
- False-positive/false-negative behavior is measurable and red-teamable before prototype expansion.

## Anti-Outcomes (What This Is NOT)
- Not a credential vault that encourages central storage of passwords, government IDs, or full identity documents.
- Not spyware, covert surveillance, doxxing, retaliation, or offensive intrusion.
- Not an autonomous law-enforcement agent.
- Not a guarantee that identity theft can always be prevented or recovered.
- Not a hard-coded dependency on one LLM, verification vendor, government agency, or reporting system.
- Not a claim that the broader Shield FoundUp is already implemented in the repository.
