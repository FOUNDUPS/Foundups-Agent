# Vote/Ballots - Outcome Definition

## User Outcome
A person can enter VOTE from the public FoundUp surface, immediately understand the candidates and political influence surrounding their local election through evidence-backed records, then choose how deeply to participate. VOTE begins as transparent civic intelligence and matures into a verified preference-signaling and governance surface that can test the same primitives later used by FoundUp SmartDAOs.

The mature experience separates four things that conventional platforms tend to collapse: identity, evidence, preference, and governance execution. RedDog/pfMALL provides the conversational and visual interface; VOTE provides the civic decision context; shared FoundUps infrastructure provides verification, tokenization, audit, and later SmartDAO governance hooks.

## Political Claim Verification Outcome - Planned
A person can move from a speech, debate, ad, or public post to the exact factual claim, its primary evidence, methodological limitations, disclosed conflicts, and a reviewable assessment. The **Bullshit Meter** adds asynchronous claim verification first, then audited scoring, caption/video overlays, and eventual live operation. An experimental 300-850 index must show coverage, uncertainty, and correction history; insufficient evidence remains unscored.

Canonical design and source: [claim-verification engine](../POLITICAL_CLAIM_VERIFICATION_AND_SCORING_ENGINE.md) and [original 012 specification](CLAIM_VERIFICATION_SOURCE_20261030.md). This is a planned feature, not part of the delivered mock-data funding chain.

## Success Metrics
- Public PoC: a first-time visitor can reach a Vote landing surface without member gating and inspect evidence-backed candidate/funding context.
- Truth: every material claim is classified as verified, inferred, or unknown and carries provenance/trail-stop information.
- Claim verification: each assessment preserves the exact quote/context, source locators, factual verdict, separate confidence, audit findings, and review/correction history.
- Internal evaluation: ten historical speeches across 2-5 public figures form a documented evaluation corpus with independent claim review and explicit coverage/abstention metrics; the corpus is not yet selected or evaluated.
- Score integrity: numerical ratings require an evaluated, versioned rubric; unknown evidence, funding conflicts, and unproven intent are not silently converted into falsehood.
- Reuse: capture, geolocation, map, alert, auth/gate, blockchain, and governance capabilities are reused from existing ecosystem components whenever architecture permits.
- Prototype: authenticated users can express non-binding preference signals through a cryptographically auditable parallel-ballot experiment without representing those signals as official election votes.
- Assurance: consented users can monitor whether official-source registration status requires attention without VOTE silently changing or submitting official records.
- Governance destination: validated signaling/voting primitives can be reused by FoundUp SmartDAOs rather than remaining Vote-only code.

## Outcome Evolution
1. **Truth** - candidate, funding, attack, influence, and factual-claim transparency, including audit of the evidence used by the verifier.
2. **Charter / preference** - user-controlled issue priorities and evidence comparison without candidate persuasion or automated endorsement.
3. **Signal** - explicit, auditable non-binding preference/mandate signals.
4. **Governance** - reusable voting-verification and mandate mechanics for FoundUp SmartDAOs.

## Anti-Outcomes
- VOTE is not an official election authority or certified public-election voting system by default.
- VOTE does not silently decide how a person should vote.
- VOTE does not convert inferred funding relationships into verified accusations.
- VOTE does not build a second pfMALL shell, second capture engine, second map stack, or second blockchain layer when the ecosystem already has a suitable reusable primitive.
- VOTE does not use private user data for political microtargeting or targeted persuasion.
- VOTE does not claim a registration record has been changed or remediated unless an authorized official-source workflow proves it.

- VOTE does not equate missing evidence with falsity, financial association with a false statement, or contradiction/repetition with proven intent to deceive.
- VOTE does not present an experimental index or ten-speech sample as a validated, comprehensive ranking of political figures.
