## Problem and objective

A congressional investigation can continue its legislative work even when federal criminal enforcement is unavailable or delayed. Where the reviewed record separately supports a possible state-law violation, Congress should have a predictable **screening and lawful referral** decision rather than treating DOJ action as the only outcome. A presidential pardon concerns federal offenses; it does not itself grant state jurisdiction or erase independent state-law elements.

## Proposed rule / committee procedure for legal drafting

1. **Trigger a documented screening decision**, not an automatic prosecutorial handoff, when committee counsel identifies credible, source-linked evidence potentially implicating an identified state statute. Record facts, contrary evidence, gaps, and whether the committee's legislative purpose and collection authority were valid.
2. **Determine competent recipient and nexus.** Identify each applicable state's offense or civil enforcement authority, territorial connection, venue, limitation periods, and whether an attorney general, local prosecutor, or regulator has original or delegated authority. A proposed network of 24 offices is an outreach target, **not** a presumption of 24 lawful recipients.
3. **Conduct a disclosure gate.** Committee officials and counsel verify governing chamber/committee rules, authorization to share, privilege, Speech or Debate considerations, privacy, protective orders, grand-jury secrecy, and witness-immunity restrictions. House rules cannot override statutes, court orders, Senate rules, or state law.
4. **Protect evidentiary usability.** Preserve provenance, chain of custody, versioned records, exculpatory or contradictory material, and an independent review. Special care is required for immunized testimony and derivative-use restrictions; do not imply that a House committee can offer criminal plea bargains or bind prosecutors.
5. **Record send / hold / close.** Require a written decision, reason, responsible official, and follow-up date. For an approved referral, use a verified secure recipient channel and transmit only approved material with a versioned inventory and delivery receipt. No protected material goes to public GitHub or public Sites.
6. **Keep decisions independent.** Receiving state authorities decide whether to investigate, compel evidence, settle, charge, or decline. A delivery acknowledgment is not acceptance or prosecution. Congress continues legitimate legislative oversight regardless of state response.

## Authority boundaries

A House resolution may establish House-internal review, reporting, custody, and referral-approval procedures subject to constitutional and statutory limits. It cannot compel the Senate or state officials to cooperate, create state jurisdiction, grant state investigative powers, or override confidentiality protections. Senate coordination requires separate Senate authorization. Interoffice protocols and secure intake agreements require each participating office's consent and applicable law. Counsel must review the proposed text against the House rules currently in force and relevant committee-specific disclosure procedures before describing it as operative.

## Witness and upward-evidence pathway

Financial and corporate records → source and custody review → voluntary or authorized witness interviews → corroborated findings → committee legislative decisions and, where legally supported, state-law screening. Witness cooperation, immunity, and proffers are distinct legal mechanisms controlled by the relevant lawful authorities. Do not describe pressure on junior staff, managers, or executives as a substitute for independent corroboration, due process, or voluntary truthful testimony.
