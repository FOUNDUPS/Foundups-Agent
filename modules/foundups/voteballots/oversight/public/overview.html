Process — build, review and lawfully refer evidence

Committees act under their own rules, preserve and independently test the record, obtain counsel and disclosure approvals, and assess whether a separate state-law matter supports a referral to a competent office. Explore the hub-and-spoke map or follow the five-stage roadmap.

Result — Congress acts; states decide independently

Congress can pursue hearings, reports, legislation and oversight without waiting for DOJ. A state attorney general, prosecutor or regulator independently determines whether to investigate or enforce applicable state law. Review jurisdiction and disclosure requirements.

STATE REFERRAL

Find the office with authority.

Congress can pursue legislative oversight even when federal prosecutors decline to act. Federal criminal contempt still goes to a U.S. attorney under 2 U.S.C. § 194. A separate state referral depends on the facts and the state's own law.

The proper recipient may be an attorney general, local prosecutor, or regulator. Counsel must match the facts to the statute, jurisdiction and disclosure rules before transfer. The receiving office decides whether to investigate, pursue a civil remedy or prosecute.
