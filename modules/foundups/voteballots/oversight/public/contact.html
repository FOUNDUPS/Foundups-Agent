    Prepare a message or voicemail

    Start with your name and town if you’re comfortable sharing them with the office. Say what oversight question you’re calling about and what response you’d like. An office may ask for a ZIP code or callback number.

    “Hello, I’m [name] from [town, state]. I’m calling about [oversight issue]. Could the senator’s office explain [specific question or next step]? Thank you.”

    Your draft stays in this page. Read it aloud when you call and share personal details directly with the office if needed.
