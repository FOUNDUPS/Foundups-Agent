PROVENANCE AND LIMITS

Three kinds of records

Direct contribution
A payment that the filer reported as its own contribution to a campaign committee. The selected records come from AIPAC PAC, committee C00797670.
Individual earmark forwarded by a PAC
An individual's contribution that the PAC received and forwarded as a conduit. An earmark is tracked separately from the PAC's own money. FEC earmark reporting guidance
Independent expenditure
Reported spending for communications supporting or opposing a candidate outside the campaign. The FEC lists United Democracy Project (UDP), C00799031 as a separate independent expenditure-only committee. AIPAC describes UDP as AIPAC-backed. FEC independent expenditure guidance

What the labels cover

Verified identifies a selected item matched to a public filing. Inferred would identify an interpretation supported by sources. This page publishes filing facts and separately sourced voting records. Unknown applies where the selection cannot support a finding or total. Missing entries do not establish zero activity.

Amendments, refunds and conduit records matter when researching totals. Each filing link downloads the electronic report. Search for the displayed transaction ID to find the exact row. Its amount is a reported line item. An official roll call establishes the recorded vote on its specific question. Questions about motive, control, coordination, legality and the origin of money require their own evidence.
