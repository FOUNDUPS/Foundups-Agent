Hasan Piker Coalition Makes the Case for Piker as Speaker of the House

Independent campaign calls on House members to consider an outside communicator and outlines civic-accountability tools being developed with FoundUps.

November 13, 2026 · Issued by HPC

November 13, 2026 — The independent Hasan Piker Coalition (HPC) is calling on members of the incoming 120th Congress to consider nominating and voting for political commentator Hasan Piker for Speaker of the House. The campaign uses hasan4speaker.com as its public address and combines political satire, creator-led organizing and a call for more accountable congressional leadership.

HPC is an independent initiative. It does not represent Piker or claim his authorization, participation or endorsement. The proposal is the coalition’s advocacy—not an announcement of a Piker candidacy.

A public-facing alternative

HPC’s case is that a prominent communicator, supported by experienced legislative, policy, legal and administrative professionals, could make congressional decisions more visible and help build public pressure for healthcare, education and housing.

The coalition invites creators, organizers and supporters to participate in its Hasan-for-Speaker effort and its SchumerOUT and JeffriesOUT campaigns. Shared views do not automatically establish coalition membership or endorsement.

HPC also challenges the influence of billionaire donors, corporate interests, AIPAC and the AIPAC-backed United Democracy Project (UDP). UDP is a separate super PAC, not a new name for AIPAC.

The House—not an online poll—chooses the Speaker

Article I, Section 2 of the Constitution gives the House the power to choose its Speaker. The House’s own historical guidance states that the Speaker need not be a sitting member, although every Speaker to date has been one. Under the House’s longstanding practice, election requires a majority of votes cast for a person by name, with a quorum present.

HPC proposes combining public communication with an experienced governing team, not replacing parliamentary procedure with an audience. Staff expertise would support the Speaker; it would not remove the office’s responsibilities or guarantee legislative votes.

Civic tools being developed with FoundUps

HPC is working with FoundUps on four distinct civic tools. Existing public information and manual-contact pages are separate from capabilities still in development.

Vote

Public discovery and research: helping people examine politicians, disclosed funding, public records and source-linked evidence. Existing work includes selected filing excerpts and manual-contact resources; broader profiles, media grids and rating features remain in development. Missing disclosure is not treated as zero funding or proof of misconduct.

Voice

Citizen action: connecting an issue and its evidence to an explicitly user-directed call or public expression. The current entry uses Vote’s manual Contact Congress page. A separate Voice service and assisted-calling features remain proposed—not advertised here as operational.

Poly Vote

A proposed module within Vote for separately verified officeholders to nominate people and express leadership or committee preferences, with private or deliberately public disclosure. Authentication, office verification and voting authority are distinct. In-app preferences would not replace official congressional proceedings.

Poly Voice

A separate planned FoundUp for consented, on-device meeting records and business-only summaries. The design keeps conversation content and structured JSON local, with an optional cryptographic commitment receipt. A receipt would not, by itself, prove that a meeting occurred. Recording, encryption and identity features remain subject to implementation and independent verification.

Participate and scrutinize

HPC invites supporters to review the proposal, question its assumptions, contribute to the public codebase and contact their House member. The House switchboard is 202-224-3121. Contact is voluntary and visitor-initiated; website activity does not cast a congressional vote.

The coalition’s objective is to turn attention into informed participation and visible accountability—not to claim guaranteed election outcomes or algorithmic reach.

About HPC

The Hasan Piker Coalition is an independent civic-advocacy and political-satire initiative advancing the Hasan-for-Speaker proposal and developing accountability tools with FoundUps. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Piker, the Democratic Socialists of America, or any public official depicted on its website.
