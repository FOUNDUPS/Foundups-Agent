THE PROPOSED CHARTER

/ STANDARD

Contribute a skill.
Keep your judgment.

These are the working principles for a future coalition. Preparing a draft does not create a commitment or grant a role.

01

Show the evidence.

Link original public records, keep facts separate from interpretation and mark missing information as unknown.

02

Respect independence.

Participation would be voluntary. Each person keeps their own voice and judgment; no individual or organization is presumed to endorse the project.

03

Make correction possible.

Invite contrary evidence, acknowledge mistakes and keep a visible correction trail for published work.

THREE WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

/ PATHWAYS

Different experience.
The same standard.

Choose the pathway that describes a contribution you would like to make. A selection is a statement in your draft, never a verified credential.

CREATORS

Explain the record.

Prepare source-linked explainers, make complex records readable or help people follow an evidence trail.

A public YouTube, Twitch or X profile is required in a creator draft. Ownership remains unverified.

PUBLIC OFFICIALS

Clarify the process.

Contribute public procedural knowledge, identify official source material or explain institutional responsibilities.

An institutional .gov page is required in an official draft. Office, authority and identity remain unverified.

COMMUNITY

Improve the work.

Help with accessibility, translation, source organization or careful review of public information.

A profile is optional. A display name is enough to prepare a community draft.

PRIVATE APPLICATION PREPARATION

/ LOCAL DRAFT

Prepare it here.

A draft is not membership. It is not submitted, reviewed or verified. Keep private details out of it; use public information and a display name you are comfortable sharing.

If local drafting does not load, you can still read the charter and prepare your own private notes. There is no online submission service.
