# Roadmap

- PoC: offline supplied-fact gate demonstration and synthetic tests only.
- Before prototype: independent owner/overlap review; explicit applicability and
  unknown states; typed source provenance/freshness/conflict checks; renewal and
  refund/credit transition tests; privacy-reviewed private storage integration.
- Before admission: current WSP_00/97 lifecycle; repository structural/aggregate
  tests; approved registration and generated manifests; installed discovery proof.
- External actions require separate existing executor and policy authorization.
  No scheduler, filing, payment, signature, bank integration or autonomous renewal
  is enabled by this roadmap.
