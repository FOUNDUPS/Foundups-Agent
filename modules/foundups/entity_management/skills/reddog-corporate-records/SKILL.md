# Red Dog corporate records

Draft framework, not installed or admitted execution. Apply current WSP_00 and WSP_97; record unavailable native gates without claiming compliance. Reuse the project's existing private Mosh Pit and records folder; do not create a parallel case ledger.

## Resolve the actual question

Identify the legal entity, jurisdiction, agency, account/program, reporting period and requested outcome from authorized private evidence. Resolve aliases and historical names before matching records. Corporate standing, filing acceptance, tax balance, federal exemption and local exemption are separate questions. A payment or incorporation certificate cannot answer all of them.

Read the latest project ledger first, then the underlying notices, submitted forms, agency acknowledgments and payment evidence. Preserve original artifacts and dates. Label observed, reported, inferred and unknown separately. A stale public listing does not override a newer authenticated agency decision without investigating the conflict.

## Common reconciliation loop

1. Inventory each obligation separately: agency/program, period, filing type, due date source, observed status, amount/currency if relevant, evidence reference and next action. Do not infer deadlines, penalties or exemption from a different jurisdiction.
2. Distinguish DRAFT, SUBMITTED, RECEIVED, ACCEPTED, REJECTED and NEEDS_INFORMATION. An upload, email acknowledgment or debit is not official filing acceptance. Capture the actual agency receipt/status, effective date and any conditions.
3. For payments, match payee, entity/account, purpose, period, amount/currency, payment date and transaction reference. Distinguish initiated, settled, agency-applied, reversed and disputed. A bank debit proves neither correct allocation nor tax clearance. Never pay again merely because a portal has not updated.
4. For credits/refunds, distinguish requested, approved, issued, received and reconciled. A credit balance is not a cash refund; an approval is not funds received. Match actual credit application or incoming settlement to the correct account/period before closing.
5. Reconcile contradictory records explicitly. Retain both sources, observation dates and the smallest missing proof. Draft an inquiry only if needed; sending it needs scoped authorization and verified recipients. Do not admit liability or waive rights as an incidental clarification.
6. Record a compact factual ledger delta: date/time, item ID, source reference, state, owner, next action and exhibit reference. Reuse existing format and preserve history. Stop a closed follow-up only after the specific outcome is evidenced; unresolved items retain a concrete trigger/date, not endless unchanged polling.

## Authority checkpoints

Reading, organizing and drafting do not authorize filing, payment, election, signature, disclosure, withdrawal, refund request or account changes. Before an external action, resolve exact entity, agency, form/version, period, material declarations, destination and authority under the current confirmation policy. Required handoffs and action-time confirmations remain mandatory; the skill grants no standing permission. Do not substitute a browser/API route to avoid a required handoff. Never automate a repeated submission while the first result is uncertain.

Use the existing private evidence owner for account identifiers, government identifiers, bank/payment information, correspondence and receipts. Never put private evidence, credentials, actual entity/account IDs or private case links in public repository files, prompts used as fixtures, or skill examples. Public code stores generic procedures only.

## Receipt and stopping rule

Report which question was answered, authoritative source/date, observed outcome, remaining exceptions and exact next step. Never collapse 'payment received', 'filing accepted', 'good standing', 'tax cleared' and 'exempt' into 'everything resolved'. Unavailable portal access is a verification blocker, not evidence of delinquency. Keep repository publication, installed skill discovery and observed runtime use separate.
