# IRS lookup boundary

Use https://www.irs.gov/ current official instructions, Tax Exempt Organization Search and the entity's authenticated determination/correspondence as applicable. Check which determination or return period the record covers; a public search result can be incomplete or dated.

Separate entity identification, recognition of exemption, ongoing filing obligations, automatic-revocation/reinstatement records and payment allocation. A pending application is not recognition. A payment is not acceptance of a return or resolution of a notice. Do not infer tax liability, eligibility or refund entitlement without the applicable current source and entity-specific evidence.
