# Japan lookup boundary

Resolve entity type and national versus prefectural/municipal authority before selecting a process. Start from official https://www.nta.go.jp/ for national tax and https://www.moj.go.jp/ for corporate registration sources; follow the specific local government's official site for its taxes or recognition.

Keep corporate registry evidence, corporate-number lookup, national tax obligations, local tax obligations and any special-status recognition separate. Preserve the Japanese label and official period on each notice; translate without silently substituting a U.S. category. Verify current instructions and local applicability, rather than transplanting D.C./IRS procedures. Portal receipts and bank debits do not establish acceptance or correct allocation.
