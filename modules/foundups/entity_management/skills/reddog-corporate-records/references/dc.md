# D.C. lookup boundary

Use current official D.C. DLCP Corporations instructions for entity records, reports and corporate standing; use D.C. Office of Tax and Revenue for tax accounts and local exemption. Start at https://dlcp.dc.gov/ and https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/ and follow their official links to the relevant service. These entry points are not evidence that any particular form, fee or deadline applies.

Verify entity type, exact reporting period and which agency issued the notice. Keep corporate reinstatement/report acceptance separate from tax clearance and local exemption. Recover the actual submitted document and official disposition before recommending another filing or payment. Do not derive local exemption from IRS recognition alone.
