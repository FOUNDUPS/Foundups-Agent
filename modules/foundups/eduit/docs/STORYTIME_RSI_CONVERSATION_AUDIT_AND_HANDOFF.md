## Evolution and child-friendly story
Use **MAMMA** (M-A-M-M-A) as the fictional child-friendly name derived from mammaliaforms, never confuse it with **Mom**. Three memorable mammal branch labels: **MAMMA 🥚** (monotremes), **MAMMA 🦘** (marsupials), **MAMMA 🤰** (placental mammals). These are branches, not sequential transformations. The story follows the placental branch toward primates, hominins and humans. Around 150 million years ago is a Jurassic fictional setting, not evidence of direct descent from a modern shrew or from a specific named fossil. Scientific map should include reviewed taxa/clades, approximate ages, uncertainty, release status and YOU ARE HERE at the opening episode. ## Learning engine — two imprinting templates
**Narrative: SEE TEXT → HEAR SPOKEN TEXT → FADE.** Short unpunctuated preschool captions, one at a time: `long long ago`, then `there were dinosaurs`, followed by the little MAMMA family/sleeping/danger story. Display image/emoji 🦕 for unfamiliar vocabulary, then briefly emphasize **DINOSAUR** with synchronized pronunciation; optional educator-reviewed syllable segments and gentle non-strobing transition. Captions and spoken words must correspond in each reviewed locale. Author event timings in **milliseconds** and measure actual speech onset, not merely speech request.

**Action: SEE PHYSICAL EVENT / HEAR FOLEY → SEE WORD → HEAR SPOKEN WORD → FADE.** For THUMP: foot/shake + low-frequency impact, visible THUMP, then audible localized spoken “thump”; CRACK, SNIFF, CRUNCH and SQUISH use the same template. Initial *unvalidated* THUMP offsets: effect 0 ms, word 80 ms, speech request 220 ms; measured speech onset/jitter are acceptance criteria. Cancel stale speech on replay, pause, tab hide and language switch; provide captions and silent mode. Phone-speaker bass must be tested; browser TTS is a prototype/fallback, not millisecond-precise production audio. Use reviewed recorded TTS assets for precise scheduling.

**Object contract:** stable concept ID, locale, approved words/audio/illustrations, cue kind, scene anchor and safe bounds, responsive scale, text size, opacity/fade, timing offsets, prominence/review state, rights, revision and assessment mapping. Learning objects must not be hard-coded into game physics. On subsequent exposure, a familiar word may become less prominent while remaining reviewable. Do not infer mastery from one success.

## Game and learning cycle
Five characters in cave; wake with multiple taps, preserve progress dots; optionally stagger sleeping Z cues and tap-resistant Zzz, with no obscured hit targets. Each rescued arrival can show **1 / one** through **5 / five**, localized word + voice + brief highlight. Vocabulary banks (five candidates each): sounds THUMP, CRACK, SQUISH, SNIFF, CRUNCH; family Mom, Dad, Brother, Sister, Baby; numbers 1–5; actions Wake, Tap, Go, Run, Safe. Use a few new focus concepts per round, retain familiar cues and spaced review. Review language and voice for EN/JA/PT/ES. Rescue awards stars *during play*. **No Earn Stars button**. Primary completion control is large, pulsing, bottom-right **Play Again**; respect reduced motion. A round counter can track successful rescue rounds 1/3–3/3, but no forced assessment gate. On replay vary approved content and seeded safe positions; slightly increase pace within accessible bounds. Ending: five rescued → descending foot → strong THUMP → soil CRUNCH → foot lifts → Everyone Safe → Play Again. Preserve non-graphic treatment. VERIFY remains a future optional/integrated recognition/matching/tracing capability, not a permanent third panel or forced test.
