# Validation and research gates

Status: plan, not completed evaluation. This document adds present-day qualification
requirements; the historical sources do not supply the necessary validation evidence.

## Claims ladder

1. Software works: approved games run, score, adapt within bounds, and preserve progress.
2. Learning value: improvement on specified objectives versus a suitable comparison.
3. Retention/transfer: benefits persist and extend to held-out tasks, not just repeats.
4. Discovery validity: repeated domain evidence supports a useful, calibrated review
   signal and improves access to suitable opportunities without unacceptable errors.

Passing a lower rung does not establish a higher one. No fixed “law of intelligence”
or diffusion-of-innovation curve is implemented as a psychometric model. The founder’s
bell-curve analogy is a hypothesis/framing device, not a measured distribution here.

## Pre-registered study design

Before enrollment, specify constructs, target populations, sampling/recruitment,
consent, comparison condition, outcome measures, exclusion/missing-data rules,
power/precision rationale, stopping rules, uncertainty reporting and harm monitoring.
Split at learner and site level where needed to prevent leakage; keep game-training
items separate from held-out evaluation. Determine sample size from the actual study,
not a promotional target. A single global sample cannot establish all cultures/devices.

## Measures and confounds

Measure learning gain, delayed retention and transfer with uncertainty. For discovery,
report calibration, false-positive/false-negative rates, referral burden and opportunity
outcomes, including precision at the real low base rate of the target phenomenon.
Consider prior exposure, language, device latency, motor/sensory access, assistance,
fatigue, socioeconomic access and motivation. Do not treat response speed as innate
ability. Assess subgroup fairness only with lawful, consented data and adequate samples.
Where those conditions are absent, report the limitation rather than claim fairness.

## Human assessment and opportunity decisions

Use independent subject-matter and educational reviewers. Provide a learner/guardian
explanation and correction/appeal route. A lack of a discovery signal never closes
access to practice or voluntary enrichment. Schools and scholarship providers make
their own decisions under their policies; EDUIT does not promise admissions or awards.

## Release gates

POC: functional determinism, bounds, accessible controls, schema rejection tests and
no external data transmission. Prototype: auth/consent/tenant isolation, replay-safe
sync, deletion/export and security review. Pilot: approved research protocol and
frozen measurement packs. Discovery: construct validity, calibration and decision-impact
evidence. Production: operational/privacy review, support, incident response and rollback.

## Prohibited shortcuts

No LLM-only genius classification; no treating synthetic examples as trial data;
no engagement metric standing in for education; no unreviewed generated test deployment;
no retroactively choosing successful outcomes; no public ranking of children.
Infant learning, parent simulation, and any medical/safety benefit require a separate
qualified research/regulatory pathway; none is enabled by this plan.
