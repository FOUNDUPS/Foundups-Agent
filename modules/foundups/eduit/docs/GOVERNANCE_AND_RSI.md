# DAE governance and governed game improvement

Status: future contract. No autonomous worker, token economy, or recursive
self-improvement capability is deployed by this package.

These preserve the founder’s direction but do not prove current platform capabilities.
Use the repository’s current meanings/contracts where historical terminology differs.

## Separate three kinds of adaptation

Session adaptation selects within an approved game/learning policy.
Content improvement proposes new packs, rules, translations or explanations.
Model/system improvement changes models or infrastructure. These require different
permissions and evidence; choosing a harder item is not proof of recursive improvement.

## Governed loop

Observe consented, purpose-limited evidence -> propose a bounded change -> create a
sandbox candidate -> test against frozen baseline and held-out material -> independent
review -> authorize exact release -> staged rollout -> monitor -> retain or roll back.
Record objective, source/version, provenance, evaluation, reviewer, authorization,
release hash, costs, outcomes and rollback target. The proposer cannot approve itself.

## Governance roles

The founder sets mission and delegates bounded authority. Educators/research reviewers
approve learning and measurement claims. Safeguarding/privacy owners control participant
risk and data use. Maintainers approve technical releases. Learners/guardians have
understandable participation, correction and withdrawal rights. Define named operators
and escalation contacts before a pilot; no appointments are invented here.

## Economics

Keep `TOKEN_DEFERRED`. No wallet is required for learning or opportunity consideration.
Do not pay children to disclose data or sell ability profiles. Any future creator
rewards, scholarship funding or DAE mechanism requires separate legal, governance
and economic review. No 2023 prices, returns or delivery schedules are reactivated.
Track accessible learning benefit and real operating costs; define any CABR calculation
through the platform’s current owner instead of inventing a second scoring formula.
