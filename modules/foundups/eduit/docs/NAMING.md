# Naming decision — EDUIT and its modules

Decision date: 2026-10-08. Scope: working product and engineering names; no legal rename,
trademark clearance, domain registration, social-account rename, or public launch.

## Decision

Keep **EDUIT** as the educational parent and stable FoundUp ID `eduit`.
Use **EDUIT Discovery** as the working label for potential-discovery and opportunity routing
within EDUIT. Use **The Experience** for the learner-facing adaptive play environment.
Keep The Tool / The Content / The Experience as the historical architectural vocabulary.

| Edguit | Speech-transcription form in this conversation | Resolve to EDUIT; do not create a duplicate entity |

## Alternatives considered

Renaming the entire FoundUp would obscure its historical identity. Creating a second
Discovery FoundUp would duplicate tenant, governance, and learner-data ownership.
Naming it after a famous genius would imply a classification the product cannot yet
substantiate. The chosen working name describes its purpose without those changes.

## Boundaries

No `discovery` token, independent learner wallet, new catalog tile, or second tenant.
`esingularity_001` continues to own the infrastructure/campaign module. EDUIT may eventually
consume compute through a documented service agreement, but does not depend on any
particular physical site. Naming clearance remains a pre-public-branding task.
