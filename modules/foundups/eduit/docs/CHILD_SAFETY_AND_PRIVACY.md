# Child safety and privacy requirements

Status: proposed safeguards and launch gates, not a legal-compliance opinion.
Jurisdiction-specific law, school rules and research requirements must be checked
with qualified reviewers before a real pilot. No compliance certification is claimed.

## Roles and permission

Separate learner, guardian, educator/reviewer, provider and operator roles. Verify
applicable guardian/organization authority rather than accepting a checkbox alone.
Use separate, revocable choices for learning records, research, external referrals
and optional likeness/voice processing. Access to learning cannot require agreeing
to sell data, share publicly, or participate in token economics.

## Prohibited in the first implementation

No infant-monitoring product, diagnosis or emergency-response assurance. No automatic
parent likeness/voice cloning, facial recognition, eye-tracking or biometric collection.
No direct stranger-to-child messages, public child profiles, public rankings, targeted
advertising, wallets, trading, gambling mechanics or paid rewards for data disclosure.
No age-equivalent/IQ/“genius” labels from gameplay. No autonomous scholarship outreach.

## Safe learning experience

Describe the AI as software, not the learner’s real parent or a substitute caregiver.
Use supportive feedback, a clear stop/pause control, and non-manipulative progression.
Make accessible alternatives available. Do not infer a disability or emotional state
from gameplay. Provide a human review channel for concerning content or decisions.
Use vetted, rights-cleared content; do not assume publicly viewable media are licensed.

## Data protection

Minimize identifiers, restrict purpose and access, encrypt data in transit and at rest
when a backend is introduced, isolate tenants/cohorts/learners, and audit access.
Publish clear retention and deletion behavior, including derived profiles, offline queues,
backups and provider-held referrals. Do not claim that a pseudonymous record is anonymous.
No child data in public GitHub, public model-training sets, blockchain, or shared WRE memory.

## Partner and incident controls

Vetting must cover provider legitimacy, eligibility, safeguarding, data-sharing terms,
conflicts of interest and contact routes. A human approves the recipient and the minimum
referral payload. Recheck permission at send-time and retain a delivery receipt.
Support withdrawal, correction, complaints, incident containment, access suspension,
notification decisions and investigation by qualified owners.

## Baby0 boundary

The historical early-learning and parent-support concepts remain documented, not erased.
They are not part of the school-age Discovery pilot by default. Developmental benefits,
infant safety, parent simulation, crib placement and health functions require independent
specialist review and a dedicated protocol. Historical advertising is not a safety test.
