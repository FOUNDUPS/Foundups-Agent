# EDUIT / eSingularity — historical application and continuity

This is a history of recorded intentions and designs, not a retrospective claim of
successful deployment. Source dates inside documents, Drive timestamps, and later
retellings are kept distinct. Full originals remain in the owner’s Drive.

## 2007: accessible education through a web portal and play

S01, the mission draft dated July 24–25, 2007, expands EDUIT as Education Using
Information Technology. It proposes free online learning in math, science, humanities,
languages, job training, and vocational certification for youth, adults, and seniors.
The delivery mechanism is an interactive web portal plus a play-learn experience.

Application now: keep ordinary-device access and lifelong learning as the mission.
A limited initial test cohort must not silently replace that broader mission.

## Initiative presentation: The Tool + The Content + The Experience

S02 is titled “Eduit eSingularity Initiative 2.2 — started 2007”; its body says version 2.3.
It has later edits. Its title alone does not date every slide to 2007.
The presentation distinguishes content-authoring tools, reusable educational media,
and a personalized autonomous play environment. It expressly includes phones, PSP,
and Nintendo DS as intended access devices. Its projected media headlines and
funding/impact figures are scenarios, not verified accomplishments.

Application now: a browser/PWA delivery layer, a rights-cleared content catalog,
and versioned game activities. Do not require custom tablet manufacture.

## 2009: no genius left behind; learning objects and scholarships

S03, the book draft carrying a 2009 copyright notice, contains the headline
“No genius left behind.” It describes objective-based video clips (OBVCs),
objective-based video modules (OBVMs), and widget eGames. Its proposed allocation
of profits includes creator rewards, prizes, and scholarships. A fictional learner
scenario explicitly describes using existing devices without buying new hardware.
The Socrates character is an avatar in that scenario, not a discovered child.

Application now: retain objective-linked gameplay and the opportunity pathway.
The exact contemporary “find a Plato/Einstein/Mozart” formulation is the founder’s
current explanation, not a verbatim passage recovered from the book.

## 2009–2010 Malaysia prize draft: discovery through interaction

S04 carries a 2009 copyright notice, mentions the 2010 Consumer Electronics Show,
and has a 2010 Drive modification timestamp. It must not be labeled wholly 2009.
It proposes identifying brilliant students in rural India through their interactions
in an intelligent learning environment. It also names GuiGame as a proposed tool
for making adaptable games tied to learning objectives.

Its testing section proposes a starting-level search: move upward after a successful
game, bracket a failure, and narrow the interval. Its network section requires core
learning to continue without internet and describes queued content, local storage,
and distributed delivery. Those are genuine historical design details; they are
not evidence that the method estimates intelligence accurately.

Application now: test a bounded placement algorithm; support cached approved game
packs and delayed synchronization; preserve discovery as a research question.
The new implementation need not reproduce the historical peer-to-peer architecture.

## 2023: teaching tablets, Baby0, OBAI, and DAEs

S05–S08 describe a teaching-tablet product, multisensory and multilingual learning,
parental interaction modeling, adaptive content, and aggregate learning feedback.
The EDUITs roadmap sequences language arts, math, then science and explicitly
connects the vision to Foundup DAEs and CABR. The Baby0 dialogue includes the
founder’s statement that the work would start as an app and discusses prototyping
on an existing iPad or Android tablet. S08 opens with existing touch-tablet hardware
plus new eSingularity software.

These sources mix founder statements, AI-generated replies, draft advertisements,
financial forecasts, and speculative developmental/health claims. They are not
clinical studies, psychometric validation, proof of product shipment, proof of AGI,
or evidence that named partnerships or funding commitments remain active.

Application now: preserve the adaptive-software and beneficial-governance intentions.
Separately gate infant use, parent likeness/voice modeling, sensors, and health features.
No guaranteed language fluency, IQ gain, scholarship, or medical benefit enters the MVP.
Historical prices, promised returns, and old delivery dates are not current offers.

## 2026: software-first EDUIT with Discovery inside it

Current founder direction: use a web app on ordinary devices; allow learners to play;
investigate exceptional abilities wherever learners live; connect consenting learners
to enrichment, mentors, schools, and scholarships; improve games through governed AI.

Current engineering decision: establish the documentation and declarative FoundUp
scaffold first. Preserve `eduit` as the parent, name the internal capability EDuIT Discovery,
and keep all runtime and validation claims at SPECIFIED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED.
The 2026 safety, evaluation, and release gates are new implementation decisions,
not claims that these safeguards existed in the old designs.
