## Retrieval and provenance rules

Source text outranks recollection for historical wording. A draft’s predictive statement
remains a prediction. AI dialogue is attributed as dialogue, not independent corroboration.
Keep source ID and the specific supporting section with every historical claim.
Do not silently modernize the source: place changes under a “current decision” heading.

## Remaining archive discovery

Located but outside this completed core review: India e3.0 prospectus; d-EDUIT business
plan and prospectus draft; another same-title whitepaper; Japanese overview/FAQ;
team manuals and governance/contract documents; magazines/PDFs and related videos.
Review these in bounded batches before making claims that depend on them.
Administrative and credential records are not product-design inputs.
Potential duplicates must be compared before merging or deleting; no archival files
were renamed, moved, overwritten, or deleted in this work.

## Public/private boundary

This public register uses source titles and bounded locators. Private Drive IDs,
full manuscripts, source contact details, family details, and access tokens are not
required for builders and are omitted. Source permissions have not been changed.
