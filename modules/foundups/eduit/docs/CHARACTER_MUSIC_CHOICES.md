## Engine and sleeve

The deterministic engine owns wake/travel, hazards, clock, score and completion.
The sleeve owns artwork and optional background music. Reuse the existing
`rig.mjs` appearance allowlist, `setAppearance`, Shorts director and narration;
do not add another engine, renderer, account, catalog or child-memory database.

The first visible character wheel offers existing MAMMA and original fantasy
MAMMA Mermaid art. Its thumbnails use those same rigs. Swiping settles on a
choice; arrows, keyboard and click are alternatives. Choices pause immediately
without resetting the round, score, rescue progress or frozen drawing. The
original video/storybook retains its MAMMA story; changing a game sleeve does
not silently regenerate a film. Future character packs need approved local art,
all existing poses, rights, safe bounds and these same mechanic invariants.

The music wheel offers Off, Carefree and Wholesome. Off is the default. Selection
alone never starts music; explicit game Play starts the chosen track quietly.
Pause, hidden page, video/story playback and a new choice stop music. Playback
denial is visible and requires explicit Play to retry; rescue taps cannot retry.
The existing impact/Foley remains separate. Music does not change physics or
claim to improve memory. No remote streams, random script URLs or tracking.
