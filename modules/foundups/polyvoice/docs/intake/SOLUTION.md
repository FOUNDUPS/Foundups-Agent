# Poly Voice — solution

Reuse qualified capture, local media and identity services; own consent, business-summary review, encrypted retention and commitment policy in Poly Voice. Do not copy the Mall, inherit social publishing or move private content to a remote transcriber.

**PoC:** one synthetic/staged meeting, one qualified device, manual capture/review, business JSON and a local commitment demonstration; independently verify private-content non-egress.
**Prototype:** separately authorized minimal receipt transport, identity/office attribution where required, replay/revision and device/key recovery tests.
**MVP:** scope and operating ownership remain to be specified from pilot evidence; not inferred from a roadmap or a deployed page.
