# Poly Voice — outcome

A participant keeps an accessible, encrypted, locally reviewable business-meeting record and can voluntarily publish a limited commitment without uploading private conversation or business JSON. A public reader can distinguish a receipt/self-attestation from independently verified meeting facts.

Success must demonstrate the declared local-data boundary, honest interruption/failure states and review/correction. No total-meeting census, guilt inference, guaranteed future source access, adoption forecast or electoral-conversion objective is claimed.
