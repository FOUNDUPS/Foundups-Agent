# JHR Research Skill

## Identity

Japan Hyperscaler Report (JHR) is the Japanese-first evidence desk for Project eSingularity.

## Trigger

Run a bounded assessment when the JHR freshness interval has elapsed. A run is an assessment, not a publication event.

## Research order

1. Primary government: METI, MIC, MLIT, Agency for Natural Resources and Energy, prefectures, municipalities, planning boards and council records.
2. Grid/power: regional utilities, OCCTO and public capacity/planning material.
3. Operators/developers: hyperscaler, colocation, construction, real-estate and infrastructure announcements.
4. Community evidence: municipal consultations, resident associations, farmers, landowners, temples/shrines, local councils, petitions, hearings and local media.
5. Media/radio: local newspapers, NHK/local broadcasters, radio programs and specialist trade press.
6. U.S. comparison only when it explains a Japanese decision or trajectory.

## Standing questions

- What changed since the previous evidence receipt?
- Is this proposed, approved, under construction, energized, operational, paused or cancelled?
- How much land, power and water is actually committed?
- Who controls the land and interconnection queue?
- What does the local government say versus the developer?
- What do residents, farmers and nearby institutions say?
- Is a local rule, zoning response or political reaction emerging?
- What does this imply for Fukui before equivalent hyperscale pressure arrives?
- Does distributed 1-20 MW COG DC architecture offer a materially different option?

## WSP 97 rules

- Never turn a search result into a fact without opening/retaining source evidence.
- Label `proposal`, `candidate`, `selected`, `approved`, `construction`, and `operational` distinctly.
- Separate company claims from government-confirmed facts.
- Significant public claims require corroboration; policy claims prefer a primary government source.
- Community response must not be inferred from one anecdote.
- No report is a valid outcome (`NO_REPORT`).
- Never manufacture a monthly issue merely because a month elapsed.
- Every published claim must bind to a source ledger entry.
- Images require provenance and attribution; do not imply ownership of third-party aerial imagery.

## Publication package

A promoted issue should include:

- Japanese headline and dek;
- 5-sentence executive summary;
- `Why Fukui should care` section;
- scale explainer understandable to a 19-year-old;
- Chiba/Inzai community-impact section when relevant;
- policy/grid/land table;
- source ledger;
- image provenance;
- SEO title/description/keywords;
- social summary and restrained hashtags;
- WSP 97 truth-boundary note.

## SEO vocabulary

Use only when relevant: `Japan Hyperscaler Report`, `JHR`, `Japan data center`, `Japan hyperscaler`, `印西 データセンター`, `千葉 データセンター`, `福井 データセンター`, `GX戦略地域`, `ワット・ビット連携`, `AIインフラ`, `COG DC`, `eSingularity`.

Avoid hashtag stuffing. Prefer 5-10 exact tags tied to the issue.
