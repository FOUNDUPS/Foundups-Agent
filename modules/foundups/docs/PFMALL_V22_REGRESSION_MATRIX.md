### v23 responsive clarification

Portrait selected TV keeps the player floating above the grid. On compact
landscape phones (height at most 500px), the inherited right-side floating
player remains at its original size and position; selected header, sorting and
video browsing reserve a separate left region. This prevents the measured
v23 candidate collision without moving the player below a header where its
200px stage would no longer fit. The new browser oracle checks actual tile,
header and control intersections, viewport bounds and a stationary player,
with initial failing evidence retained. HPC fixed scope is unchanged.
The selected-view logo returns to Mall with saved state; leaving selected TV
restores its ordinary Member Portal destination. This does not change HPC or
Vote/Contact return identities.
