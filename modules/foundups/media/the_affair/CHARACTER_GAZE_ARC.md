## Plate contract

- Generate seven authored performances, not a mechanical rotating-head effect.
- Generate each state as an individual keyframe. A single seven-panel generation is not an acceptable production method for nuanced facial acting.
- Preserve adult scale, upper-corner placement, title-safe center, phone crop, and visual world.
- Jensen moves **sky → horizon → Alice**. Alice moves **past below → self-possession → Jensen**.
- The period-specific Alice or memory below must occupy the destination of adult Alice's gaze in each state.
- The season image below carries chronology; the adult faces carry interpretation.
- The title is live HTML. It peels left-to-right from blur into sharp focus on first entry only.
- The POC uses layered season states and swipe/tap navigation; it does not overlay a persistent season selector.
- The adult/background layer leaves the center safe zone empty. The separate center poster carries the memory at which Alice is looking.
- Double-tap (or keyboard `Enter`/`Space`) flips the center poster to its concise prospectus back without moving the adult faces or title.

## Acceptance gates

- Every adjacent state changes at least three of: eyes, head/chin, brows, mouth/cheeks, posture, counterpart reaction.
- Any three consecutive states may not reuse the same brow-and-mouth performance.
- State 3 must contain Alice's first visible restrained smile; state 4 must read as professional composure; state 5 as strategic testing; state 6 as knowing confidence; state 7 as calm, unreadable eye contact.
- The unlabeled sequence must remain orderable at 150 px panel width. A reviewer should place at least six of seven states correctly and identify at least five intended emotional beats.
- If the last three faces appear substantially the same, the sequence fails even when the gaze angles differ.

These are dramatized visual states, not factual claims about private thoughts or motives.
