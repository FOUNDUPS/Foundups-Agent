# The Huang Affair | 黄乐

Working abbreviation: **THA**. Image inventory, private archive recovery, and cover review: [`media/README.md`](media/README.md).

Status: **prototype FoundUp; private seven-state PWA under review**.

*The Huang Affair* is a fictionalized limited-series concept about love, access, and compute. Public presentation must separate sourced chronology, open questions, and dramatized narrative. It must not present private motives, relationships, covert conduct, or causation as established facts.

## Current prototype

Sprint 1 proves one interaction only:

- one locked cinematic environment;
- seven layered season states assembled from responsive adult/background plates, separate center posters, and live copy;
- adult Alice remains upper-left and Jensen remains upper-right while their authored gaze, expression, and head angle carry the character arc;
- the period-specific Alice and season imagery beneath them change with each memory;
- the live title peels into the title-safe center on initial load;
- swipe/tap advances through the seven posters without a persistent numbered season selector;
- the middle season poster is a separate two-sided card: double-tap flips to a concise prospectus back; double-tap again restores the front;
- a movie-ticket CTA on the prospectus back routes to the existing FoundUps member-access owner and must fail closed at login;
- mobile-first layout with a responsive desktop composition;
- explicit fictionalized-concept framing.

The image plates remain in the private deployment repository. The public monorepo contains the implementation contract and source shell, not private research masters or unpublished generated portrait assets.
