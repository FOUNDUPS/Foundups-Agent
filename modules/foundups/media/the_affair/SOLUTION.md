# Solution

Use a living poster rather than a conventional scrolling landing page:

1. Keep one cinematic environment and stable layout.
2. Use seven phone and seven desktop background plates containing only the environment and the two adult performances; keep the center title/card safe zone empty.
3. Keep adult Alice upper-left and Jensen upper-right. Author gaze and expression as performance: Alice moves from the memory below toward Jensen; Jensen moves from the sky and future toward Alice.
4. Place one separate scalable season-poster card in the safe center. The seven card-front images carry the period-specific memory scenes and can be changed without regenerating the adult/background plates.
5. Reveal the live title once on entry with a restrained horizontal peel; do not replay it on every swipe.
6. Support swipe and tap-edge navigation on phones plus arrow keys on desktop. Do not place a persistent numbered season selector over the poster.
7. Double-tap the center card to flip from poster front to a live HTML prospectus back; double-tap again restores the front. Keyboard `Enter`/`Space` provides the accessible equivalent.
8. Keep the back compact: documented-history framing, dramatized question, principal players, and only the opening/finale pitch. Do not invent episodes 2–5.
9. Present a movie-ticket CTA on the back that routes to the existing FoundUps member/login owner and fails closed.
10. Preserve the existing deeper Site experience behind this landing layer. Do not replace or duplicate it.

This layered composition is simpler than baking copy into artwork: expensive character plates stay stable while the center image, text, flip content, and ticket route remain editable.
