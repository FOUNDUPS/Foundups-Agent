# Solution

Use a living poster rather than a conventional scrolling landing page:

1. Keep one cinematic environment and stable layout.
2. Crossfade between seven full-frame plates.
3. Change only head angle, gaze, and restrained expression across plates.
4. Update one central aperture with chapter number, title, one-line hook, and evidence status.
5. Support swipe, tap, previous/next, and keyboard navigation.
6. Add episodes, dossiers, and access only through later accepted gates.

This is simpler, more legible on phones, and easier to reuse than maintaining separate phone and desktop narratives.
