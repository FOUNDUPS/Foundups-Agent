## Sprint 1 — current proof of concept

**Outcome:** 012 can open the private prototype on a phone and move through seven coherent visual states.

Acceptance:

- exactly seven states;
- adult Alice remains upper-left and Jensen remains upper-right;
- the environment, title-safe center, and adult scale remain locked while memory imagery below changes by chapter;
- gaze, head angle, and expression follow the authored character arc;
- state 1 begins with Alice looking into her past and Jensen toward the sky/future; state 7 reaches direct eye contact;
- the title peels into its safe zone once on entry without an opaque title panel;
- swipe and tap-edge navigation work on phones; arrow keys work on desktop;
- no persistent numbered season selector obscures the poster;
- the adult/background art and center-poster art are separate responsive layers;
- double-tap flips the center poster to a concise prospectus back and back again;
- `Enter`/`Space` provides the accessible flip equivalent;
- the back contains documented/dramatized framing, principal players, and opening/finale pitch only;
- the movie-ticket CTA reaches the verified existing FoundUps member/login surface and grants no access itself;
- the existing deeper Site work remains intact behind the landing;
- phone view does not require horizontal scrolling;
- desktop uses the same state model, not a second product;
- reduced-motion mode remains usable;
- no unsourced allegation appears as fact.

Not in Sprint 1: full episode content, episodes 2–5 titles, full dossiers, protected scripts, new membership code, payments, animation loops, public launch, or custom-domain activation.

## Later gates

Sprint 2 refines only the opening and finale presentation already exposed on the prospectus back; episodes 2–5 remain unnamed until editorial approval. Sprint 3 hands protected pitch/script requests to the existing FoundUps member-access owner. Each slice must be accepted before the next begins.

## Continuity

Every accepted interaction is folded into the reusable media workflow. Rejected experiments are recorded as evidence, not promoted into templates.
