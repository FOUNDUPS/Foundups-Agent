## Sprint 1 — current proof of concept

**Outcome:** 012 can open the private prototype on a phone and move through seven coherent visual states.

Acceptance:

- exactly seven states;
- the environment and composition remain locked;
- head angle/gaze changes are the only plate differences;
- state 1 begins apart and state 7 reaches direct eye contact;
- center aperture and chapter label update deterministically;
- swipe, tap, previous/next, number keys, and arrow keys work;
- phone view does not require horizontal scrolling;
- desktop uses the same state model, not a second product;
- reduced-motion mode remains usable;
- no unsourced allegation appears as fact.

Not in Sprint 1: episodes, dossiers, scripts, membership, animation loops, final season copy, public launch, or custom domain activation.

## Later gates

Sprint 2 adds only the seven center tiles. Sprint 3 exposes only the opening and finale montage for each chapter. Sprint 4 adds a concise producer pitch and routes script requests through the existing FoundUps member-access owner. Each slice must be accepted before the next begins.

## Continuity

Every accepted interaction is folded into the reusable media workflow. Rejected experiments are recorded as evidence, not promoted into templates.
