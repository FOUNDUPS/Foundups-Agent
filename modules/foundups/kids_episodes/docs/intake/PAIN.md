# Kids Episodes — Pain

## Primary pain
Turning a story into a coherent series of short educational episodes requires separate work for story breakdown, continuity, visuals, music, caption timing and export, making small repeatable episodes cumbersome.

## Severity
Frequency: recurring production. Impact: material creator effort. Alternatives: general video editors plus generation/music services; comparative usability and cost are not yet measured.

## Target user
Adult creators, educators and caregivers preparing child-directed learning videos. Children are the audience, not assumed account holders.

## Evidence
012's explicit workflow request and supplied example are the current product signal. No market-size, learning-effectiveness or demand study is claimed.
