# Kids Episodes — PoC scope

## Minimum proof
Use supplied local visuals and music with a small episode JSON contract to render a real 15-second MP4 and subtitle artifact through the YouTube module.

## Included
Draft/final presets, portrait/landscape, ordered shots, timed captions, explicit soundtrack, validation and reproducible test fixtures.

## Deferred
Verified live generation, automatic word alignment, live trend collection/scheduling, public deployment, monetization and automatic publication. These remain requested product stages, not discarded requirements.

## Completion evidence
A decoded output, measured duration and requested dimensions, audible soundtrack, visible captions and negative-input tests. A reviewed real educational sample is required before prototype promotion.

## Trust wedge
A locally inspectable episode export with clear evidence of what was rendered and which steps still require review.

## Product boundary clarification

This local proof validates the renderer component only. The target FoundUp must accept a story, plan and maintain linked episodes, and autonomously orchestrate verified assets and captions. These are still required next stages. EDUIT game linkage is a future objective, not an implemented result.
