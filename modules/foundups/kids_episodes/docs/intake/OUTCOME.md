# Kids Episodes — Outcome

## User outcome
An adult creator supplies a story or teaching intent and receives a coherent sequence of linked 15-second educational episodes with consistent characters, learning progression, music and synchronized captions. The creator can review/revise the sequence and export draft or final versions. Future episodes can connect to objective-matched EDUIT learning games.

## Success metrics
- Story and episode identities, continuity and learning progression survive retries and revisions.
- Real export is 15 seconds within one-frame/container tolerance, decodes and has the requested aspect/resolution.
- Every spoken/sung teaching phrase has verified caption timing and correct spelling.
- One correct learning objective and one safe activity fit the episode without incoherent cuts.
- Asset provenance, rights and review results are retained.

## Anti-outcomes
A rendering fixture is not proof of educational effectiveness. Volume, watch time and trend ranking do not replace learning quality. A draft is not a publishing authorization.
