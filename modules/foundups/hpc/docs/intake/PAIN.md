# HPC pain

## Primary pain point and audience

People looking for political-media commentary and its underlying context must cross independent feeds, reposts and civic information sites. 012's manual clipping and tagging on Move2Japan is evidence of that workflow. The PoC addresses a curated audience interested in that topic.

## Pain severity

Frequency, population impact and the adequacy of competing tools remain unmeasured. The observed operator workflow includes repeated manual discovery and attribution.

## Problems and evidence

012 reported manual clipping and fragmented discovery. The source catalog has a stale snapshot relative to the current uploads. Source provenance, attribution and a working link to civic resources are testable in the PoC.

## Risks and hypotheses

Duplicate or misleading edits, missing context, content rights and embed restrictions require moderation and source review. Platform-controlled engagement, sparse preference signals, spam, bots, coordinated manipulation, privacy and identity risks require evaluation before participation. Mall federation and public hosting are incomplete, and Voice's telephony, consent and recovery remain separate implementation gates.
