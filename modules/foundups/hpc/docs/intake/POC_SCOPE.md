# HPC PoC scope

## Minimum useful PoC

Open `hpc.foundups.com` into the shared SchumerOUT collection. Use curated, public Move2Japan Shorts and preserve their title, source and publication metadata. Include the independent-satire disclosure and a microphone link to Vote's manual Contact Congress resource.

## Included and excluded

Include compact playback, immersive controls, browse restoration, global discovery and a protected app handoff. Reserve other campaigns, submissions, ratings, calling, account changes, token economics and new hosting approval for separate work.

## Success criteria and trust wedge

A signed-out visitor can play a verified Short on the HPC host, collapse to its tile, follow its source, open the disclosure, reach the contact resource and return to the same position. An invited member can discover HPC in the FoundUps Mall and open the scoped collection after admission. Source attribution and the distinction between commentary, satire and official civic resources are visible.
