# HPC PoC scope

## Minimum useful PoC

Open the existing Mall directly into a public SchumerOUT grid. Use curated, public Move2Japan Shorts and preserve their title, source and publication metadata. Include the independent-satire disclosure and a microphone link to Vote's manual Contact Congress resource.

## Included and excluded

Include compact playback, immersive controls, browse restoration, global discovery and a protected app handoff. Reserve other campaigns, submissions, ratings, calling, account changes, token economics and new hosting approval for separate work.

## Success criteria and trust wedge

A signed-out visitor can play a verified Short, collapse to its tile, follow its source, open the disclosure, reach the contact resource and return to the same position. An independent visitor to the global Mall can discover HPC. Source attribution and the distinction between commentary, satire and official civic resources are visible.
