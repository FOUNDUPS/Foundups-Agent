# HPC solution

## Core solution

Use Play FoundUps Mall to organize attributed political-media Shorts into topic collections. Vote supplies civic context and official-contact links. The separately governed Voice experience can become a destination through a qualified interface.

## Key capabilities and delivery stages

- **PoC:** Scoped public grid of existing Move2Japan Shorts, source and editorial context, independent-site disclosure and Vote's manual contact resource.
- **Prototype:** Nominations, verification, moderation, revised or cleared seven-level preferences and improved discovery.
- **MVP:** Governed submissions, scalable moderation, verified preference aggregation and operational, consented Voice integration.

## Differentiation and technical approach

Use durable source identities and shared discovery with a purpose-specific scope. Reuse the Mall's registry, route, grid, player and gesture owners. Review data provenance, rights, consent, privacy and moderation before adding contribution or preference events.

The recovery uses the shared three-column field and full-width inline player, with immersive expansion and a stable return anchor. The shared player reports provider errors and offers an explicit playback or source fallback. The [shared incident](../../../pfmall/MEDIA_PLAYBACK_INCIDENT_HPC.md) owns media evidence and the remaining device gates.
