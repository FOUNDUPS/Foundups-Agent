# HPC outcome

## User outcome

People can find, share, nominate, discuss and evaluate political-media content in an open, decentralized community. They can follow a clip back to its creator and context, express a preference, and reach civic information or communication tools from the same discovery journey.

## Success measures

- A visitor can identify a clip's source and publication date and open the original.
- A visitor can reach official-contact resources and resume their browsing position.
- Later stages can measure reviewed nominations, provenance coverage and preference participation with privacy-preserving reports.

## Anti-outcomes

Editorial opinion, factual verification and economic value remain distinct. Participation and support stay voluntary. The community has an independent identity and a separate relationship to Vote and Voice.
