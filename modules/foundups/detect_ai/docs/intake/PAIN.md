# Can You Detect AI? - Pain Definition

## Primary Pain Point
People lack calibrated feedback about their ability to recognize automated conversation. Builders need evidence that a small, affordable model works for a specific conversational role, rather than assuming the biggest model or highest general benchmark score is the right choice.

## Pain Severity
- Frequency: potentially daily for active online communicators; weekly or per release for builders. These frequencies are adoption hypotheses, not measured demand.
- Impact: proposed medium/high trust and model-selection cost; willingness to participate or pay is unvalidated.
- Alternatives: adequate for basic entertainment because Human or Not exists; suitability for our combined evaluation and reward requirements needs comparative validation.

## Target User
Curious adult players; small-model and conversational-agent builders; researchers needing consented interaction evidence; communities running structured tournaments. Later educational use needs a separate age-appropriate design and review.

## Specific Pains and Response
- Confidence without feedback -> classification plus probability, outcome and calibration history.
- Incomparable model claims -> common protocol, pinned configurations, separate model/judge tracks and reported uncertainty.
- Small-model selection by reputation -> measured latency and cost alongside conversational results.
- Unhelpful agent habits -> a separate Red Dog rubric for relevance, instruction-following, concise acknowledgment and evidence honesty.
- Low-quality, unconsented training data -> explicit purpose-specific permissions, provenance, quality review and held-out evaluation.
- Incentives that reward volume rather than value -> verified contributions and capped reward eligibility, not tokens for every message.

## Pain Evidence
The 2023 Human or Not study demonstrates participation in the basic format; it does not prove demand for this FoundUp or tokenized rewards. Controlled Turing-test research shows results depend on the tested system and prompting. 012's corrections in this conversation are concrete product requirements for Red Dog, not a population study.
