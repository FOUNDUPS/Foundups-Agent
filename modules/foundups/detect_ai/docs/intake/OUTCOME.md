# Can You Detect AI? - Outcome Definition

## User Outcome
People learn how accurately they can distinguish human-controlled conversation from AI, and how well their confidence matches reality. Builders compare small open models and frontier services on the same declared task, protocol, and cost basis. Agent judges are evaluated separately from human judges.

## Ultimate Vision
A community-supported evaluation FoundUp: an accessible game generates consented evidence; transparent benchmarks help builders select economical conversational systems; verified contributions may qualify for governed FoundUp rewards. Red Dog learns from curated failures of relevance, brevity, continuity and instruction-following, not simply from winning an imitation contest.

## Success Metrics
- Valid completed rounds, return participation, queue delay and abandonment, separated by assignment arm.
- Human and agent judge balanced accuracy, confidence calibration and sample counts.
- Conversational-model human-judgment rate by fixed model/prompt/runtime configuration, with uncertainty and human baselines.
- Measured cost per valid evaluated round and response latency; local hardware/quantization disclosed.
- Held-out Red Dog task-relevance and instruction-following improvement without increased unsupported claims.
- Consent/withdrawal compliance, abuse rates, disputed rewards and moderation response.

Targets and sample thresholds are in the gate documents. No results exist yet.

## Anti-Outcomes (What This Is NOT)
- Not proof of consciousness, quantum behavior, universal intelligence or AGI.
- Not an identity-verification or accusation service.
- Not undisclosed impersonation outside a consented game.
- Not a guaranteed income product, token sale, betting pool or automatic payout loop.
- Not permission to publish 012's private Gmail, contacts, conversations or entrepreneurial archive.
